Privacy policy

Draft · last updated 5 October 2026

CrossCart is a Chrome extension and web app that lets you save products from online stores into carts. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google. Used only to sign you in.
  • Products you save: when you press “Add to CrossCart”, the product’s name, link, price, currency, picture, the size or colour you picked, and which cart you put it in. Used to show your carts and keep them in step across your devices.
  • Your settings: your preferred currency and light or dark mode.
  • Waitlist: if you join the “pay once” waitlist, how many items and stores were in your cart and its total, so we know what to build.

What we don’t collect

  • The extension looks at the page you’re on only to recognise a product and show its button. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press “Add to CrossCart”.
  • We don’t record your browsing history, what you look at without saving, or anything you type on store websites.
  • We don’t take payment details. Paying stores through CrossCart isn’t available yet.
  • We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and carts (servers in Ireland, EU).
  • Google handles “Sign in with Google”.
  • open.er-api.com provides exchange rates. It receives no personal data.
  • Sentry receives error reports when something breaks in CrossCart (servers in Germany, EU): the error message, where in our code it happened, and the CrossCart page address without anything after “?” or “#”. No email, account details or store pages you visit.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: which page was opened, the browser and rough country. No cookies, nothing that identifies you, and nothing from the extension.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the web app (“Delete account” under Sign out). That permanently removes your account, every cart and item, and your waitlist entry. Signing out of the extension also clears the carts stored in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Contact us at hello@crosscart.one.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
