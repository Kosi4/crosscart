What CrossCart is

CrossCart saves products from online stores into carts you can see in one place, with a running total. It does not sell anything, and it doesn’t buy from stores for you yet. To buy, you follow each product’s link and check out on the store’s own website.

Prices and details

Prices, sizes, pictures and stock come from the stores’ pages at the moment you saved them and may change. Totals, shipping and fees shown in CrossCart are estimates. The store’s own checkout is always the final word.

Your account

Keep your sign-in to yourself. You can delete your account at any time from the web app. We may suspend accounts used to abuse the service or other people’s websites.

Stores and other websites

CrossCart isn’t connected to, or endorsed by, the stores you save from. Their products, prices and policies are theirs.

No guarantee

CrossCart is provided as it is, free of charge, while it’s new. We work to keep it accurate and available but can’t promise it always will be, and we aren’t responsible for purchases you make on other websites.
